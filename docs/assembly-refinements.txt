P1 mount 1.1 — assembly refinements and physical calibration

Editable helper: assembly-refinements.scad, used by the main frame source.
All dimensions are millimeters. These nominal digital additions do not establish
vehicle retention, service temperature, clamp load or cable/connector suitability.

SCREW STACK
The original 2 mm bearing webs, slot datums and +/-3 mm shank travel remain.
Do not select a spacer from the author's trial washer count: actual washer
thicknesses and usable insert depths were not supplied.

Four flat calibration shims are supplied at 0.5 / 1 / 1.5 / 2 mm, arranged left
to right in the shim-set export. Each has a 14 x 20 capsule outline and a
6.8 x 12.8 capsule bore, preserving the full 6 mm screw travel. The bearing
recess is 16 x 22, giving 1 mm nominal radial perimeter clearance. The bore
has short separate entry relief; both bearing faces stay planar.

Stack order from below: screw head, METAL flat washer, selected printed shim,
original 2 mm web, node insert. The shim is not a replacement for the metal
washer. The 0.5 mm shim is intentionally a thin, fully supported fit specimen;
it is not an unsupported structural tab. Do not assume shim thickness is exact
until printed thickness, compression and creep are measured.

ar_screw_engagement = measured_under_head_length - web - measured_metal_washer
                     - selected_shim.
ar_screw_stack_report reports NOT CHECKED until screw length, washer thickness,
front and rear insert depths and required minimum engagement are supplied.
A provisional 0.5 mm bottoming margin is exposed as a parameter, not a verified
hardware specification. Measure both inserts independently. Tightening must
clamp the node before bottoming while preserving the hardware's specified
minimum thread engagement. Check tightening torque with the actual material.

CABLE ROUTE AND RESTRAINT
The reference-only cable centerline preserves the original connector tail bend,
loops behind and left of the pylon, then approaches the photo-derived left jack
axially. It has C1 joins, 25 mm N-type / 15 mm stock minimum source-sampled
curvature radius, and source-sampled centerline lengths ~288.37 / 211.08 mm.
The N-type route extends rearward to Y about -174; stock to -148.2. These are
reference wire envelopes, not additions to the printable frame footprint.

The 300 mm catalog cable length datum and rigid termination lengths have not
been measured. In particular N-type leaves little nominal centerline margin;
the actual cable may require a different route or a longer cable. Stock has
surplus length whose final service loop remains unverified. Do not claim that
every millimeter of a purchased cable has been routed or its assembly proven.

A variant-specific, printable saddle is integrated with the left rear foot/rail
shoulder. The 16 x 20 capsule root lofts smoothly from Z8 into a rounded 14 x 20
working head. N-type nominal cable center is (-88.823,-86.627,33.225); stock
(-84.731,-64.393,35.268). The body stays inside the original X=-100 boundary.
The open cradle follows the curved wire centerline with 1 mm radial allowance.
Its crest is 0.8 mm below the nominal cable axis so the tie bears on the cable
instead of bridging on two high shoulders. Retention is from the separate tie,
not an untested integral snap catch.

Nominal tie: 2.5 mm wide, no more than 1.5 mm thick. The transverse 3.4 x 2.2
capsule passage has R1.1 outline and a separate 0.35 mm entry flare. The actual
supplier/grade, insertion force and tightening force remain unknown. Tighten
only enough to restrain motion; do not crush coax or force a bend at a crimp.

EXPORTED-GEOMETRY CHECKS
tools/check_assembly_helpers.py regenerates only helper geometry and tests the
result independently. audits/assembly-helpers.json records actual bounds and
topology. Four shim pieces and each saddle are watertight positive solids.
A 2.5 x 1.5 rectangular tie envelope swept across the complete exported eye
gives an empty Boolean interference for both variants. Central vertical
ligament sampling (169 columns within a 3 x 3 mm region) gives 3.063 mm N-type
and 2.930 mm stock minimum material between tie eye and cable cradle. These
are local samples, not global minimum thickness or strength validation.
The final frame's cuts/union still need their own checks; standalone helper
checks do not automatically transfer to a changed assembled export.

PHYSICAL CHECKS STILL REQUIRED
Print shims flat and a small saddle/foot region in the intended material and
orientation. Verify measured thickness, actual screw/washer stack, clamp,
thread engagement, socket access at both slot extremes, and creep under load.
Thread the actual tie both ways, inspect its mouth contact, install/remove the
cable without pinching, and confirm the tie does not collapse the coax.
Verify the complete cable and both connector/tool envelopes against the node,
magnet nuts, roof, rear removal grip and antenna. Confirm release/cleanup access.
Full-frame export and slicer checks are separate from these helper tests.

Use the actual material grade and outdoor/temperature qualification chosen for
the full mount. A provisional A1 PETG prototype is not a quiet substitution for
the original ASA/P1S service-material choice. No print or printer operation was
performed as part of this digital helper work.
