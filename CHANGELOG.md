# Changelog

## 1.1 (proposed, digitally checked)

- Round frame-window roots and upper edges while retaining mounting interfaces.
- Add variant-specific cable-tie saddles, nominal cable-route references, stock-bore lead-in and stock-pylon fit coupon.
- Add optional screw calibration shims without choosing an unmeasured screw stack.
- Normalize exported boundaries at float32 precision and validate reopened meshes.
- Include matched before/after renders and corrected, sliced A1 fit-prototype projects.
- Preserve stock fill coverage with a conservative 50 mm/s workaround; retain custom settings on Bambu Studio GUI import.

Physical printing, roof conformity, service temperature, cable fit and road retention remain untested for this revision.

## 1.0

Initial release: one-piece magnetic roof frame for the SenseCAP Solar Node P1 in N-type and stock-antenna variants, with five fit-test coupons.
